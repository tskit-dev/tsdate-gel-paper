Data for the chromosome 17 inversion analysis, provided by Anastasia Ignatieva:

- `chr17q21.31_time_plot.csv`: Relate-based age estimates for the 17q21.31 inversion by genomic
  region, from Ignatieva et al. (2025), _Molecular Biology and Evolution_ 42:msaf190,
  https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msaf190 (also available at https://github.com/a-ignatieva/dolores-paper).
- `donnelly_et_al_table_2.csv`: 17q21 inversion marker SNPs from Table 2 of Donnelly et al. (2010),
  _American Journal of Human Genetics_ 86:161–171.
